Simit Panel – Privacy Policy

Last updated: 30 October 2026

Simit Panel is a personal desktop tool that runs only on its owner's computer (localhost). It has no server, no user accounts and no analytics.

What data is used

  • Access tokens returned by TikTok, YouTube and Instagram after the owner logs in.
  • Basic profile information (username / display name) to show which account is connected.
  • The videos, titles and descriptions the owner chooses to upload.

How it is stored

Tokens and upload history are stored only on the owner's own computer. Nothing is sent anywhere except directly to the official TikTok, YouTube and Instagram APIs when the owner presses "Send".

Sharing

No data is sold or shared with third parties.

Deleting data

The owner can disconnect an account in the panel at any time, which deletes its tokens, and can revoke access in the TikTok, Google or Instagram account settings.
